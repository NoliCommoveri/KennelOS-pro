KennelAssistant

Let a helper log weights, milestones, medications and notes from their own phone — no account, no access to your contacts, sales or finances. You send them a curated copy of your dogs; their entries come back here for you to review before anything is written.

Companion Furever Assistant

Connection

KennelAssistant passes files through your own Dropbox — the same connection Import/Export uses as a backup destination. Connect on either page and both are set.

Your helper's copy

Your helper only ever sees what you last sent, so send again after adding dogs or a new litter. This does not back up your records — use Import / Export for that.

Last sent: —

Their entries

Everything your helper logged, previewed before it's written — the same dry-run every import in KennelOS gets. Entries you've already brought in are skipped, so checking twice is harmless.

Set up your helper's phone

Open KennelAssistant on their phone, sign into your Dropbox once, and add it to their home screen. It works offline and holds its own entries until they send them.
